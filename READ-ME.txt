## 1. Diagnostic ka jawab: Filing 4 ka tax 0 **sahi** hai

Aap ki Filing 4 (cmvrz1tse004o2, Salary) ki asli figures:

  taxable income        2,400,000
  tax withheld          162,000
  tax payable           0
  calculation line      TY2026-149-SALARY-2M2-3M2: base 2,400,000 → tax 162,000
  packet 1009           2,400,000
  packet 64020004       162,000        (Tax Collected / Deducted)

Yani income par tax **162,000** bana, aur wo 162,000 employer ne section 149 ke
tehat pehle hi kaat liya tha. Is liye jo baqi bacha wo **0** hai. Return ke hisab
se ye bilkul theek hai — "tax payable 0" ka matlab tax nahi bana, nahi; matlab
**kuch dena baqi nahi**.

Maine pehle jo kaha tha ke "162,000 aana chahiye tha" — wo ghalat tha. 162,000 tax
HAI; payable us ke baad 0 hai.

## 2. Do cheezein jo diagnostic me "0" dikha rahi thin (bug nahi, design)

- `INCOME RECORDS (0)` — sirf freelancer/services route income records banati hai.
  Salary route apni figure income selection + documents se leti hai.
- `TAX CREDITS (0)` — app withholding ko credit table me nahi likhti; wo draft ke
  `taxWithheld` par aur packet ki row **64020004** par jati hai.
- `CALCULATION LINES ... -credit 0 = payable 0` — ye do columns (`taxCreditApplied`,
  `taxPayable`) app kabhi likhti hi nahi, is liye har line par 0 hain. Mere diag
  script ne unhein print kar ke uljhan paida ki. Ab script unhein nahi chhapati;
  sirf "base → tax (surcharge)" dikhati hai aur saaf likhti hai ke withholding
  draft aur packet par hoti hai.
